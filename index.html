LeadOps

LeadOps is the private tool Brandz Tech uses to find and organize business leads. It finds local businesses that have no website of their own, keeps their public business contact details, and helps our team follow up with them.

How it uses Google

LeadOps connects to the Google account of its operator and creates one Google Sheet there. New leads are added to that sheet, the operator edits it online, and LeadOps reads those edits back when asked to.

LeadOps can only open the files it created itself. It cannot see anything else in that Google Drive.

Who can use it

LeadOps is an internal tool with no public sign-up. Only the Brandz Tech operator signs in to it.
