Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains what LeadOps, the internal lead tool run by Brandz Tech, does with information.

Google account data

LeadOps asks Google for one permission: access to the Google Drive files it creates. It uses this to create one spreadsheet, add leads to it, and read the edits made in it. It cannot open any other file in the Google account.

The sign-in token that allows this is kept on a private server run by Brandz Tech. Anyone who signed in can remove access at any time from their Google Account permissions.

We do not sell this data, share it with others, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

LeadOps’ use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Business contact details

LeadOps collects public business details from sources such as Google Maps, search results and public business pages. These include business names, types, locations, phone numbers, email addresses, website links and social media pages.

Brandz Tech uses these details to contact businesses about its services, and to keep track of those conversations.

Your choices

If we contacted your business and you do not want to hear from us again, reply “unsubscribe” to our email. To ask what we hold about your business, or to have it corrected or deleted, write to contact@brandz.tech.

Keeping and protecting data

We keep lead details while they are useful to our outreach, or until someone asks us to remove them. We keep a blocked address on our do-not-contact list so that it is not contacted again. Access to LeadOps is limited to its operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
